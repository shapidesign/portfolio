### 9. Project Pages — Inconsistent Case Study Structure

No Gatekeeping and Small World Problems have a clear Problem/Solution structure. The others don't. Standardize all six with this format:

```
THE BRIEF
[What was asked, or what problem existed]

THE INSIGHT / APPROACH  
[What you noticed or decided, and why]

THE SOLUTION
[What you made and the key design decisions]

THE RESULT (if applicable)
[Outcome, who uses it, what changed]
```

Pages that most need this: Keeping It Clean, Animal to Logo, Digital Handprint.
